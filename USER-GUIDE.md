# KoW Companion — User Guide
## v4.4.0 TEST

### Recommended workflow
1. **Inventory** — enter and save all resources currently held.
2. **Officer** — select each Officer and save current progress.
3. **Stars** — check current Star level, target and Star resources.
4. **Development** — enter unlock, Skill Strand and Training progress.
5. **XP** — enter current level, target level and XP Books held.
6. **Progress** — verify MAXED, IN PROGRESS and NOT STARTED status.
7. **Releases** — review the recurring schedule and future cost forecasts.
8. **Planner** — analyse current and future needs without spending resources.
9. Use **Save Progress** after important changes.

## Inventory
Inventory is shared across the app. It includes Legendary Officer Badge Chests, Legendary Officer Badge Selection Chests, ORV, SRV, Universal Legendary/Epic/Elite Badges, Stars by rarity, XP Books and Individual Officer Badges.

A Legendary Officer Badge Chest gives **1 Universal Legendary Badge**. A Selection Chest can give **1 Universal Legendary Badge** or, for an eligible seasonal Officer, the published ORV value.

## Officer
Use Search, Season, Rarity and Role to select an Officer. Officer names and game data remain unchanged. Each Officer keeps an individual progress profile while shared Inventory remains available when switching Officers.

Original Legendary Officers cannot use ORV or SRV. Eligible seasonal Legendary Officers can use them according to published costs. Epic and Elite Officers use their own rarity resources.

## Stars
Star resources are separated by rarity. Legendary Star thresholds are 500, 1,000, 6,500, 25,000 and 65,000, totalling **98,000** to reach 5★. SRV is only for eligible seasonal Legendary Officers.

## Development
Skill Strands unlock by Current Star Level: 0★ = Strand 1, 1★ = Strands 1–2, 2★ = Strands 1–3 and 3★+ = all four. Level 1 is free when a Strand becomes available.

- **Legendary**: Unlock 10 + Skills 690 + Training 900 = **1,600 Badges**.
- **Epic**: Unlock 10 + Skills 440 + Training 4,500 = **4,950 Badges**.
- **Elite**: Unlock 10 + Skills 440 + Training 18,000 = **18,450 Badges**.

## XP
Enter current level, target level and XP Books held. The app calculates XP held, required and shortfall. The total to reach Level 70 is **199,646,700 XP**.

## Progress and Compare
Progress classifies Officers as MAXED, IN PROGRESS or NOT STARTED. A MAX Officer has 5★, Level 70, is unlocked, has all four Skill Strands at Level 5 and Training 180. Compare uses the same saved data.

## Advanced Planner
The Planner is preview-only. It does not spend resources or change saved progress. MAXED Officers are excluded from recommendations.

Available sessions include Saved Officers, All Officer Sessions, the Next Officer Session, or a specific recurring session: October — Rally/Garrison, January — Infantry, April — Tanks, July — Tank Destroyers. Season and Type can be combined.

Future scenarios are clearly marked **PROJECTED**. Future ORV/SRV values remain **FORECASTS** until real costs are published.

## Planning Readiness Dashboard
Saved future scenarios show Badge, Star and XP readiness. Overall readiness uses the limiting resource. Priority Target identifies the scenario needing the most attention and Suggested Next Action identifies the next resource to save.

## Releases
Releases shows the recurring annual calendar and future Officer cost forecast. Forecasts never replace officially published costs.

## Settings, language and backup
Choose English, Français, Deutsch or Italiano. The selected language is saved on the device. Reset App returns the language to English.

**Export App Backup** creates a JSON backup of local data. **Restore App Backup** reloads a previous backup including Officer progress and shared Inventory.

## Translation QA
The v4.4.0 multilingual gate checks the rendered interface and selected User Guide. ORV, SRV, XP, MAX, TEST, Officer names and explicitly retained game terms are not treated as translation errors.
